# Person profile template

Copy this into `People/Verified_Name/Profile.md` and fill it with sourced facts. Remove unused sections. Do not invent answers. Follow [[memory/people/README|People guidance]].

## Who they are

- **Name and aliases:** as the owner or they spell it.
- **To the owner:** what they are to the owner, in the owner's words, dated. A shared surname is not kinship.
- **Where they are:** the city they live in or are in now, dated.
- **Shared activities or projects:** brief, linked to the owning project notes.

## Identifiers

Every address, number or platform id that means this person, one per line, each with the date and source it was confirmed. The engine resolves who is speaking by matching these against the session, so an identifier that is not here cannot be matched. Where a platform has both, record its permanent id, not the handle: handles can be renamed.

- <Platform>: <permanent id> (@handle; confirmed <date>, <source>)
- Email: name@example.com (confirmed <date>, <source>)
- Phone: +1 555 000 0000 (confirmed <date>, <source>)

## Contact details

For each route: channel and exact address or account ID; who supplied it (the owner, the person's account, or message headers); date and last check; the owner's account in that exchange, if known; preferred channel or style only when supported.

## Useful context

Dated facts that help the owner with this relationship, plainly, each with its source.

## History

The Profile stays under 300 words and holds only what is stable. Every dated interaction, claim and request goes in `History.md` beside this file, newest first, one line each: `- YYYY-MM-DD, <channel>: <what they said or asked>; <how it went>.` Open commitments link to `agent/Tasks.md` or the owning hub. End the Profile with a wikilink to that History note.
